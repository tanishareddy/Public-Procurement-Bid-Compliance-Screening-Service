OFFICE MEMORANDUM

Subject: Relaxation of Norms for Startups and Micro & Small Enterprises (MSEs) in Public Procurement on Prior Experience - Prior Turnover criteria.

1. The undersigned is directed to refer to this Department OM of even number dated 25th July, 2016 on the above subject and to state that representations have been received from various Startups regarding operationalization of relaxation of prior turnover and prior experience criteria.

2. In this regard, it is clarified that all Central Ministries/Departments/Central Public Sector Undertakings (CPSUs) may relax condition of prior turnover and prior experience in public procurement to all Startups (whether MSEs or otherwise) recognized by Department of Industrial Policy and Promotion (DIPP) / Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), subject to meeting quality & technical specifications.

3. It is further clarified that procuring entities shall ensure that criteria of prior turnover and prior experience are relaxed in all public procurements in respect of all DIPP/DPIIT recognized Startups, except in cases where procurement involves public safety, health, critical national security, or specialized equipment where prior experience is technically indispensable. In all normal commercial, IT, consulting, supply and works contracts, this relaxation must be mandatorily extended.

4. Bidders claiming relaxation under this order must submit a valid Certificate of Recognition issued by DIPP / DPIIT. Free-text or self-declarations without a valid certificate reference shall not be entertained.

5. This issues with the approval of Finance Secretary.
