NOTICE INVITING TENDER FOR SUPPLY, INSTALLATION, COMMISSIONING AND MAINTENANCE OF IT HARDWARE AND NETWORKING INFRASTRUCTURE

Section 2: Instruction to Bidders and Eligibility Criteria

Clause 2.1: Eligibility Criteria
The bidder must satisfy the following mandatory qualification criteria. Bids failing any mandatory condition shall be summarily rejected prior to technical evaluation.

Clause 2.1.1: Average Annual Financial Turnover
The Bidder must have an average annual financial turnover of at least INR 5,00,00,000/- (Rupees Five Crores only) during the last three financial years (FY 2020-21, 2021-22, and 2022-23). Audited balance sheets and profit & loss statements certified by a Chartered Accountant along with UDIN must be enclosed with the technical bid.
Failure to demonstrate minimum INR 5 Crores turnover shall result in immediate disqualification.

Clause 2.1.2: Past Experience and Track Record
The Bidder must have completed at least 3 (three) years of continuous commercial operations in supplying enterprise IT infrastructure to Central or State Government Departments, PSUs, or Autonomous Bodies as of the bid submission deadline. Bidder must submit copies of work orders and completion certificates.

Clause 2.1.3: Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) / Bid Security
All bidders must furnish a mandatory Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) of INR 5,00,000/- (Rupees Five Lakhs only) through Demand Draft, Bank Guarantee, or RTGS/NEFT in favor of "NICSI New Delhi".
Exemption from submission of EMD will be considered only for Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs) holding valid Udyam Registration as per Government of India guidelines.

Clause 2.1.4: Consortium and Joint Ventures
Consortium bids or Joint Ventures are not permitted for this tender. The bidder must submit the bid independently as a single prime bidder.

Clause 2.1.5: Country of Incorporation and Compliance
The bidder must be registered and incorporated in India under the Companies Act 2013 or LLP Act. Foreign bidders or overseas entities are eligible only through their 100% owned Indian subsidiaries.
